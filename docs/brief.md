# Project Brief

## Project Name
Chromapages Marketing Website

## Project Summary
Chromapages needs a marketing website that acts as the studio’s primary sales asset. The site should clearly explain what the business does, who it serves, why it is different, and how qualified visitors can take the next step.

The project is not just about having an online presence. It is about creating a website that improves positioning, trust, lead quality, and conversion.

## Background
Many service businesses have websites that either look acceptable but do not convert, or function well enough technically but feel generic and low-trust. Chromapages is positioned to solve that gap by combining performance, conversion thinking, process clarity, and ongoing support.

The website must reflect that value proposition in a way that is easy to understand for business owners and decision-makers.

## Primary Goals
- turn qualified traffic into discovery calls
- improve trust and clarity for new visitors
- create a stronger premium market position
- support referrals, outbound outreach, SEO, and paid traffic
- establish a scalable foundation for future landing pages and vertical pages

## Secondary Goals
- showcase work and credibility
- explain services and engagement models clearly
- reduce time spent manually explaining the business on calls
- filter out poor-fit leads earlier
- support future case study and content marketing efforts

## Problem Statement
Without a strong marketing website, Chromapages relies too heavily on one-to-one explanation and manual sales effort. That creates inefficiency, weakens positioning, and makes it harder to build a repeatable pipeline.

The site needs to absorb part of the sales burden by answering key questions, establishing trust, and guiding the right people toward contact.

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience
Growth-minded SMBs and local businesses that:
- want more leads, calls, bookings, or sales
- need a better-performing website
- want a more credible online presence
- value a partner with a clear process

### Secondary Audience
Startups and founders that:
- need a marketing website or landing page
- may need an MVP site or lightweight product presence
- want support beyond launch

## Ideal Visitor Mindset
The ideal visitor is already aware they have a digital problem or growth opportunity. They are not casually browsing. They are trying to find a provider they can trust to improve their web presence and help them get better business results.

## Core Message
Chromapages builds modern websites and web apps that help businesses get more customers.

## Key Differentiators
- built to convert
- fast on mobile
- clear process
- launch-ready QA
- tracking and measurement
- support after launch

## Success Metrics

### Primary KPIs
- discovery call bookings
- qualified contact form submissions

### Secondary KPIs
- CTA click-through rate
- case study engagement
- service page engagement
- scroll depth on key pages
- bounce rate improvements on primary entry pages

### Conversion Events To Track
- `view_page`
- `click_cta`
- `form_start`
- `form_submit`
- `phone_click`
- `booking_complete`

## Strategic Outcome
The website should make Chromapages feel like a serious, premium studio with a real system behind it. It should help the business attract better-fit clients, improve sales efficiency, and create a strong foundation for long-term growth.
