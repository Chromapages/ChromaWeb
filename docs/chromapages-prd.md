# 1. Product Overview

## Purpose

The Chromapages website is the primary business development platform for
a premium web design and development studio serving growth-minded
service businesses.

The website must position Chromapages as a strategic digital
transformation partner, not a generic agency, template provider,
low-cost website vendor, or full-service marketing agency.

The website exists to:

-   Explain the gap between business quality and digital presence.
-   Establish trust with qualified buyers.
-   Demonstrate premium design and technical capability.
-   Convert qualified prospects into project conversations.
-   Support a structured sales process.
-   Create a scalable content foundation through Sanity CMS.

## Positioning

**Category**

Premium web design and development studio for service businesses.

**Tagline**

Digital Design Elevated.

**Core narrative**

Your business has evolved. Your website should show it.

**Core proposition**

Chromapages builds premium websites and digital products for service
businesses designed to earn trust, guide customer action, and launch
without chaos.

## Differentiation

Chromapages competes through:

-   Premium digital experience quality.
-   Commercial clarity.
-   Performance discipline.
-   Controlled launch process.
-   Continued improvement after launch.

The website must communicate:

> A premium digital result delivered through a controlled, launch-safe
> process.

# 3. Target Users and Personas

## Primary ICP

Established service businesses with:

-   Validated offers.
-   Customer acquisition needs.
-   A website that no longer reflects business maturity.
-   A premium investment mindset.
-   A real decision-maker involved.

Typical internal guideline:

\$500K--\$5M revenue range.

This is not a public qualification requirement.

## Avoid

-   Lowest-price shoppers.
-   Template-only projects.
-   Brochure-only requests.
-   Unfunded startups.
-   Unlimited revision expectations.
-   Guaranteed-result expectations.

# 7. Non-Functional Requirements

## Performance

Target:

-   Strong Core Web Vitals.
-   Optimized images.
-   Fast navigation.
-   Minimal unnecessary scripts.

## Accessibility

Requirement:

WCAG 2.2 AA.

Must include:

-   Keyboard navigation.
-   Visible focus states.
-   Color contrast compliance.
-   Reduced-motion support.
-   Semantic HTML.
-   Accessible forms.

## SEO

Requirements:

-   Metadata system.
-   Structured content.
-   Sitemap.
-   Robots configuration.
-   Canonical URLs.
-   Open Graph support.
-   Industry landing pages.
-   Insight publishing.

## Hosting

Recommended:

Next.js deployment environment such as Vercel.

# 8. Proof and Claims Policy

## Core Rule

Evidence before adjectives.

Every objective public claim requires evidence.

## Prohibited

Never publish:

-   Fake testimonials.
-   Placeholder logos.
-   Unsupported metrics.
-   Fake dashboards.
-   Fictional outcomes.
-   Unverified rankings.
-   Guaranteed revenue claims.
-   Guaranteed conversion claims.

## Case Study Rules

All work must clearly state classification.

Concept work must remain concept work.

Owned brands must not be presented as client proof.
