# User Personas

## Purpose
These personas define the main types of users the Chromapages marketing website needs to serve. They are not demographic exercises for their own sake. They exist to help the project stay aligned with real buyer motivations, hesitations, and desired outcomes.

---

## Persona 1: Growth-Minded Local Business Owner

### Snapshot
**Name:** Marcus  
**Role:** Owner / Operator  
**Business Type:** Local service business or small multi-service company  
**Company Size:** 3–20 employees

### Goals
- get more qualified leads
- look more credible online
- stop losing opportunities because the website feels outdated or weak
- make it easier for customers to contact or book
- find a provider who can guide the process clearly

### Pain Points
- current website looks dated or generic
- mobile experience is poor
- traffic is not converting well
- previous freelancers or vendors were inconsistent
- limited time to manage a messy website project

### What He Needs From The Site
- clear explanation of what Chromapages does
- confidence that the studio understands business results, not just visuals
- proof that the process is organized
- simple path to start a conversation
- reassurance that support exists after launch

### Objections
- “How much is this going to cost me?”
- “Will this actually help me get more leads?”
- “How long is this going to take?”
- “Am I going to have to do all the work myself?”

### Decision Drivers
- trust
- clarity
- credibility
- proof of outcomes
- confidence in the process

## Persona 2: Startup Founder Or Operator

### Snapshot
**Name:** Alina  
**Role:** Founder / Early Operator  
**Business Type:** Startup, SaaS, early-stage digital business  
**Company Size:** 1–10 employees

### Goals
- launch a credible web presence quickly
- communicate the offer clearly to customers or investors
- build a site or product-facing presence that feels serious
- work with a partner who can move fast without chaos
- create a strong base for future marketing or product growth

### Pain Points
- current site is too basic or incomplete
- growth depends on presenting the product more clearly
- internal resources are limited
- technical and marketing decisions are fragmented
- past vendors focused on aesthetics without enough strategic thinking

### What She Needs From The Site
- strong positioning and message clarity
- confidence in execution
- a sense that Chromapages can support both marketing and product-facing work
- clear next steps
- confidence that the build will not become a time sink

### Objections
- “Can they actually handle something beyond a simple brochure site?”
- “Will this be flexible enough as we grow?”
- “Do they understand conversion and product thinking?”
- “How much guidance will I need to provide?”

### Decision Drivers
- competence
- speed with structure
- strategic thinking
- flexibility for future growth
- trust in delivery quality

## Persona 3: Marketing-Led Business Decision Maker

### Snapshot
**Name:** Danielle  
**Role:** Marketing Manager, Growth Lead, or Ops-Driven Decision Maker  
**Business Type:** SMB, professional services firm, growing service company  
**Company Size:** 10–50 employees

### Goals
- improve website conversion performance
- support campaigns with stronger landing pages
- reduce friction between marketing and website execution
- find a dependable external partner
- make reporting and campaign performance easier to understand

### Pain Points
- existing site is hard to update
- landing pages are inconsistent or slow to launch
- the current vendor is reactive, not proactive
- internal stakeholders want better results from traffic
- there is not enough confidence in the current web setup

### What She Needs From The Site
- clear understanding of Chromapages service scope
- evidence of process maturity
- confidence that the studio understands conversion and measurement
- proof that the partner will be organized and dependable
- a straightforward CTA to start a qualified conversation

### Objections
- “Will they be responsive?”
- “Can they support ongoing changes?”
- “Do they understand performance and tracking?”
- “Will they make my life easier or harder?”

### Decision Drivers
- operational reliability
- clarity of deliverables
- ongoing support
- measurable thinking
- professional communication

## Shared Patterns Across Personas

### What All Personas Want
- a credible partner
- clear outcomes
- simple next steps
- confidence in process
- reassurance that the project will not become chaotic

### What All Personas Fear
- wasting money
- hiring the wrong provider
- getting stuck in a slow or confusing process
- ending up with a site that still does not perform
- being left unsupported after launch

### What The Website Must Communicate To All Of Them
- Chromapages understands business outcomes
- the process is clear
- the work is trustworthy
- the offers are understandable
- the next step is easy and low-friction
