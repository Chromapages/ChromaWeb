# Chromapages Marketing Website PRD

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** Chromapages Marketing Website  
**Brand:** Chromapages  
**Tagline:** Digital Design Elevated.  
**Website Type:** Marketing website / lead generation website  
**Primary Goal:** Turn qualified visitors into booked discovery calls  
**Secondary Goals:** Showcase work, communicate offers clearly, establish premium positioning, and support future SEO and landing page expansion

### Purpose
The Chromapages website should function as a premium sales asset, not just a digital brochure. It needs to communicate that Chromapages builds fast, modern websites and web apps that help businesses get more customers. It should reinforce the studio’s differentiation: performance, conversion, process clarity, and support.

---

## 2. Business Objective

Chromapages needs a website that:

- Attracts growth-minded businesses looking for websites, landing pages, web apps, and ongoing support
- Filters out low-fit, price-shopping leads
- Converts qualified visitors into discovery calls
- Creates trust fast through positioning, work, process, and proof
- Supports outbound, referrals, SEO, and paid traffic
- Establishes a scalable foundation for future vertical brands and landing pages

## 4. Target Audience

### Primary Audience
Growth-minded SMBs and local businesses that need:
- better lead generation
- a more premium online presence
- a site that performs better on mobile
- a more reliable digital partner

### Secondary Audience
Startups and founders needing:
- marketing websites
- MVP websites or portals
- landing pages for validation or paid traffic
- long-term design and development support

### Audience Characteristics
Best-fit leads typically:
- have a real offer
- want more leads, bookings, or sales
- care about performance and business outcomes
- value a clear process
- are willing to invest in strategy, QA, and tracking

Not-fit leads:
- pure lowest-price shoppers
- template-only expectations
- disorganized clients with no approval bandwidth

## 8. Homepage Requirements

The homepage should function as the primary conversion page and sales narrative for the brand.

### Homepage Section Order

1. Header / Navigation
2. Hero
3. Social Proof
4. Services Overview
5. Featured Work
6. Process
7. Why Chromapages / Differentiators
8. Pricing Snapshot
9. FAQ
10. Final CTA
11. Footer

### Homepage Section Details

#### 1. Header / Navigation
**Purpose:** Fast orientation and clean access to key pages

**Includes:**
- Logo
- Nav links: Services, Work, Process, Pricing, About, Contact
- Primary CTA: Book A Call

**Behavior:**
- Sticky on scroll
- Mobile menu with clear CTA

---

#### 2. Hero
**Purpose:** Communicate value immediately and drive first action

**Requirements:**
- Clear headline
- Supporting subheadline
- Primary CTA: Book A Call
- Secondary CTA: View Work
- Optional proof strip beneath hero copy

**Hero should communicate:**
- what Chromapages does
- what outcome the client gets
- why it is credible
- what action to take next

---

#### 3. Social Proof
**Purpose:** Reduce skepticism early

**Can include:**
- client logos
- testimonial snippets
- trust metrics
- short proof statements
- platform or capability references

---

#### 4. Services Overview
**Purpose:** Show the main offer architecture clearly

**Featured services:**
- Marketing Websites
- Landing Pages
- E-Commerce
- Web Apps / MVPs
- Ongoing Support

**Each block should include:**
- service title
- one-line promise
- 2–3 outcome bullets
- CTA to learn more

---

#### 5. Featured Work
**Purpose:** Show proof of execution and relevance

**Includes:**
- 3–6 featured projects
- short summary
- industry or project type
- optional result or transformation
- CTA to full work page

---

#### 6. Process
**Purpose:** Reduce uncertainty and show structure

**Recommended steps:**
1. Discovery
2. Strategy
3. Build Planning
4. Production
5. QA
6. Launch
7. Support

**Goal:** Show that projects are structured, predictable, and managed clearly

---

#### 7. Why Chromapages / Differentiators
**Purpose:** Explain why this studio wins versus cheaper or fragmented options

**Core pillars:**
- Built To Convert
- Fast + Reliable
- Clear Process
- Ongoing Support

---

#### 8. Pricing Snapshot
**Purpose:** Set expectations without turning the homepage into a rate sheet

**Approach:**
- show 3 high-level engagement models or ranges
- link out to full pricing page
- reinforce fit and scope logic

Suggested models:
- Launch Build
- Landing Page Sprint
- Retainer

---

#### 9. FAQ
**Purpose:** Handle objections before conversion

**Topics to cover:**
- timeline
- pricing
- what’s included
- how revisions work
- whether Chromapages can help with copy
- whether an existing site can be improved
- whether the site will support SEO and ads
- what support looks like after launch

---

#### 10. Final CTA
**Purpose:** Close the page with one focused action

**Includes:**
- closing headline
- short reassurance copy
- CTA button
- optional secondary contact method

**Includes:**
- logo

## 18. QA / Definition of Done

The website is considered ready when:

- responsive across key breakpoints
- forms validated and tracked
- performance reviewed on key pages
- accessibility pass complete
- launch checklist complete

Additional QA should cover:
- navigation
- links
- CTA consistency
- forms
- SEO metadata
- analytics firing
- cross-browser and cross-device checks

---

## 19. Risks

### Main Risks
- weak or generic copy
- not enough proof/case studies
- too many service messages on one page
- unclear differentiation
- underbuilt contact and qualification flow

### Mitigation
- keep homepage focused
- prioritize proof early
- use strong service hierarchy
- build a real qualification layer into forms
